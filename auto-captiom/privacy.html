FORGE Privacy Policy

FORGE is designed local-first. The built-in account system stores account information, a password verifier and projects on the device.

Plaintext passwords are not stored. Passwords are protected using a unique salt and PBKDF2-SHA-256 key derivation.

Imported media and local projects stay on the device unless you explicitly use an external or cloud service. Optional AI services may process media according to the provider you choose.

You can delete your local account and its projects from Account settings.
